About / Experience

Digital creator with a builder's mindset.

I'm Ron Jamelle V. Amancio, a Computer Engineering student at Cebu Technological University. My work spans short-form video editing, social media management, Blender asset creation, and small web/app projects.

As a freelance video editor and digital creator, I've produced content for YouTube Shorts and TikTok using Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, and Alight Motion — with a focus on captions, motion graphics, transitions, and retention-focused editing.

I also build prototypes and digital tools, including a business ordering website, a personal finance tracking concept, and a delivery/task management app. Alongside creative work, I've managed online communities and run a small computer-parts buy-and-sell business.
